About This Forum

Asynchronous discussion between members of Team Lane Management Systems 1. Threads are listed newest first. Each thread covers one topic and carries a status so that open questions are easy to find. No threads have been posted yet.

How to Post

  1. Open forum.html in your local clone of the repository.
  2. Copy the template at the bottom of this file's source, marked THREAD TEMPLATE, and paste it directly below the NEW THREADS GO HERE marker so the newest thread appears first.
  3. Fill in the title, your name and role, the date, the status, and the body of the post.
  4. To reply to an existing thread, add an <li class="reply"> entry to that thread's reply list rather than starting a new thread.
  5. Update the thread's status when it changes, so open questions stay easy to spot.
  6. Commit with a message naming the thread, then push.

Thread statuses

  • Open awaiting discussion or a decision
  • Customer blocked pending an answer from the customer
  • Resolved decided, kept for the record
